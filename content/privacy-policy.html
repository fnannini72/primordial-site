This policy explains what personal data primordial-protocol.com collects, why, on what legal basis, who else receives it, and what rights you have. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and Italian data protection law (Legislative Decree 196/2003 as amended). Short version: this site collects as little as it can. Analytics run only if you say yes, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

2. What data is processed, and why

a) Technical data when you visit

Like every website, the server that hosts this site receives your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time of the request. These server logs are used only to deliver the site, keep it secure and diagnose faults.
Legal basis: legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
Retention: as set by the hosting provider for security logs, normally a few weeks, unless needed to investigate an attack.

b) Cookies and your consent choices

A small consent banner asks what you allow. Your choice is stored in a few technical cookies so the banner doesn't ask again on every page. These cookies are necessary and need no consent.
Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation to record consent (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
Details on each cookie are in the Cookie Policy. You can change your choice at any time from the Cookie Policy page or, on desktop, from the "Manage consent" tab.

c) Audience statistics (only with your consent)

If, and only if, you accept "Statistics" in the banner, the site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure, in aggregate, how many people visit, which pages they read, which device they use and how they arrived (for example from a search engine or social media). If you decline, the Google Analytics script is never loaded.
For visitors in the EU, Google states that it does not log or store IP addresses: they are used for an instant to estimate an approximate location (country, region, city) and then discarded. The data is not used for advertising, and Google signals and ad personalization are not enabled.
Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
Retention: no more than 14 months for user-level data. Aggregated reports carry no personal data.

d) Newsletter (Substack)

The newsletter runs on Substack. When you type your email into the subscription form on this site, you are submitting it directly to Substack, Inc. (USA), which manages the mailing list, sends the emails and handles unsubscribing. For the operation of its platform Substack acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, which also covers how it transfers data outside the EU. As the publisher, Fabrizio Nannini can see the email addresses of subscribers and basic engagement data in the Substack dashboard and uses them only to run the newsletter.
Legal basis: your consent, given when you subscribe (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link.
The home page also shows recent article previews. The images in those previews are loaded from Substack's content servers, which therefore receive your IP address as any web server would.

e) When you write by email

If you write to an address published on this site, for example to ask about courses, coaching or collaborations, your name, email address and whatever you choose to include are used to reply and, if you ask, to prepare an offer.
Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or legitimate interest in answering correspondence (Art. 6(1)(f)).
Retention: for as long as the conversation is relevant, and in any case no longer than 24 months after the last exchange, unless a contract follows. Contract and invoicing records are kept for the 10 years required by Italian tax law.
Please do not send health information by email unless it is necessary. If you sign up for a course, retreat or coaching program, you will receive a specific privacy notice for that service.

f) Links to other platforms

This site links to YouTube, Spotify, Instagram and Substack, but it does not embed their players or tracking pixels. When you click a link and leave the site, the platform you land on applies its own privacy policy.

Some providers (Google, Substack) may process data in the United States. Such transfers take place only on the basis of a European Commission adequacy decision, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies, or of the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the Commission (Art. 45–46 GDPR).

5. Your rights

Under Articles 15–22 GDPR you can, at any time and free of charge:

  • ask whether data about you is processed, and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict processing;
  • receive the data you provided in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

To exercise any of these rights, write to info@primordial-protocol.com. You will get a reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. You can also complain to the authority of the EU country where you live or work.

6. Other things you should know

  • No automated decision-making or profiling with legal or similarly significant effects takes place.
  • Providing data is voluntary. Without an email address you can't subscribe to the newsletter or get a reply, but you can read everything on the site.
  • This site is not directed at children under 14, the age of digital consent in Italy, and does not knowingly collect their data.
  • Data is protected with HTTPS encryption, up-to-date software, restricted administrative access and regular backups.

7. Changes to this policy

If the way data is handled changes, for example when new services are added, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Significant changes will be announced on the site.
